### `flash/data/midi/*.mid` (Mutopia Project)

Demo music for the SMF player (`flash/app/tool/smf_player.app.rb`). All five
files come from the Mutopia Project, which states a licence per file; only
files whose own metadata says `Public Domain` were taken. Renamed on import,
otherwise unmodified.

`scale.mid` and `tempo_change.mid` in the same directory are **not** third
party: they are generated by `tool/midi/test/make_test_midi.rb`.

| File | Title | Composer | Engraver | Edition the engraving follows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bach_polonaise_bwv117a.mid` | Polonaise in F major, BWV Anh. 117a | J. S. Bach | Steven McDougall | Bach-Gesellschaft Ausgabe 1851-1899, Band 43 (1894) |
| `bach_sheep_bwv208.mid` | Schafe koennen sicher weiden (Sheep May Safely Graze), from BWV 208 | J. S. Bach | Jeremy Visser | Breitkopf & Haertel, 1881 |
| `joplin_entertainer.mid` | The Entertainer (c. 1902) | Scott Joplin | Chris Sawer | Reproduction of the original 1902 edition |
| `joplin_bethena.mid` | Bethena (1905) | Scott Joplin | Magnus Lewis-Smith | Original manuscript |
| `old_100th.mid` | Old 100th (original version, 1551) | Anonymous | Peter Chubb | Geneva Psalter, 1551 |
