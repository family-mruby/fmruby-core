### `flash/usr/share/sounds/midi/*.mid` (Mutopia Project)

Demo music for the SMF player (`flash/app/tool/smf_player.app.rb`). All five
files come from the Mutopia Project, which states a licence per file; only
files whose own metadata says `Public Domain` were taken. Renamed on import,
otherwise unmodified.

`scale.mid` and `tempo_change.mid` in the same directory are **not** third
party: they are generated by `tool/midi/test/make_test_midi.rb`.

### `tool/web/assets/cyberpunk-street.png` and `flash/usr/share/backgrounds/bg_cyber_*.png`

The desktop wallpaper of the cyberpunk theme.

- **Title**: Cyberpunk Street Environment (Version 1, PNG)
- **Author**: Luis Zuno (Ansimuz)
- **Source**: https://ansimuz.itch.io/ - the pack's own `public-license.pdf`
- **License**: CC0 1.0 - the pack states: "All assets included in this package
  are licensed under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0) license, which means you
  can use them freely in any project, whether personal or commercial, without
  the need for attribution. There are no restrictions on use, modification, or
  redistribution of these assets."
- **Modifications**: `tool/web/assets/cyberpunk-street.png` is the original
  608x192 file, unmodified. The three `bg_cyber_*.png` are made from it by
  `tool/web/gen_backgrounds.py`: the right-hand side is cut to bring the
  picture to the screen's shape (the artwork loops, so it is the same all the
  way across) and what is left is scaled with nearest-neighbour. Nothing is
  drawn on top and nothing is invented.
- **Not included**: the pack's other layers, its Aseprite and PSD sources and
  its music are not in this repository.

### `flash/usr/share/sounds/voice/mahjong/default/*.wav`

The mahjong table's declarations (リーチ, ポン, チー, カン, ツモ, ロン), played
by `flash/app/game/mahjong/mahjong_sound.rb` when someone declares. Shipped
with `CREDITS.md` in the same directory; the app shows the credit
「声: Open JTalk (Mei, CC BY 3.0)」 in the lobby while they are played.

- **Voice**: HTS Voice "Mei" version 1.5 (`mei_normal.htsvoice`), released by
  the MMDAgent Project Team (http://www.mmdagent.jp/). Copyright (c)
  2009-2018 Nagoya Institute of Technology, Department of Computer Science.
- **Source**: the MMDAgent sample script `MMDAgent_Example-1.8.zip`
  (2018-12-25), https://sourceforge.net/projects/mmdagent/files/MMDAgent_Example/MMDAgent_Example-1.8/
  (zip SHA-256 `f702f2109a07dca103c7b9a5123a25c6dda038f0d7fcc899ff0281d07e873a63`,
  voice SHA-256 `b19e8e5ddef4d9d9559d654f395818e2bb3c0bbdbbca533f96086de21ac5db2b`).
- **License**: CC BY 3.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  (the voice's own README and COPYRIGHT.txt).
- **Synthesis**: Open JTalk 1.11 and the dictionary open-jtalk-mecab-naist-jdic
  1.11 (both BSD-3-Clause; Debian bookworm `1.11-3`). Neither is shipped.
- **Modifications**: none after synthesis. The files are generated by
  `tools/mahjong_voice_default.rb` (`rake voice:mahjong_default`: 16-bit mono
  16000 Hz, +12 dB), which reproduces them byte for byte.
