# Image Viewer: look at a picture

The Image Viewer (/app/tool/image_viewer.app.rb) shows one PNG or BMP
picture. A double click on a picture in the File Manager opens it here.

- The picture is shown as large as the window allows at a whole multiple
  (x2, x3 ...). One larger than the window is shown smaller by a whole
  factor (1/2, 1/3 ...), so all of it is in view.
- The transparent colour (number 01, and the transparent parts of a PNG)
  is shown as a grey and white checkerboard.
- The line at the bottom has the file name, the size (width x height) and
  how large it is shown.

The bar on top

- Open (Ctrl+O): choose another picture.
- Open in Sprite Editor (E): edit this picture in the sprite editor.
- < and > (the Left and Right keys): the picture before and after this one
  in the same folder, by name.

Pictures up to 65,536 pixels (256 x 256, for example) can be opened.
